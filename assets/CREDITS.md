# Original game art

The 1stprts contributors created the Orra Basin terrain, crewed structures, vehicles, sprites, lettering, faction markings and radio cues. The models and cues can be regenerated with `tools/make_art.py` and `tools/campaign_art.py`. The radio dialogue was written for this game. The six recordings were synthesized and radio-filtered with `tools/crew_voices.py` using Carnegie Mellon University's Flite and the CMU US RMS, SLT and AWB voices. [Flite](https://www.festvox.org/flite/)
