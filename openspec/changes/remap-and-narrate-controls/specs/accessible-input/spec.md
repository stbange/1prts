## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Configure and operate command controls

Players SHALL be able to remap inputs, adjust sensitivity, inversion, toggles and haptics, and navigate session and crew actions sequentially. Menus SHALL expose their focus, role and state through accessibility APIs or narration.

#### Scenario: Make command controls accessible

- **WHEN** the commander changes a binding and uses a single sequence to commission the mill
- **THEN** the prompts match and the accessible command reports a single acceptance.
