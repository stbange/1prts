# Explain the basin campaign through its crews

## Why

The terraces and floating towns have different reasons to control the same crossing. Players need to hear the stakes before spending the first load of alloy.

## What Changes

Provide a skippable dispatch from a named crew member in each operation, summarize the final handover and retain completion times and accomplishments.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- The `field-command` delta adds the watch briefing requirement.

### Modified Capabilities

- `campaign-continuity` covers carry eligible assets through mission results.

## Impact

External briefings, campaign ledger, in-world help and victory debrief. The delta extends the existing campaign-continuity requirement.
