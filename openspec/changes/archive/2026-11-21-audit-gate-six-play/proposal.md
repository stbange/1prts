# Audit Gate Six play

## Why

The opening encounter allowed a lone section to finish before the first opposing crew could reach the room. A commander who pressed an order during a crew change also had to press it again.

## What Changes

- Measure complete runs, rendered command response, recognition and retry for repeatable seeded commander policies.
- Allow one affordable production handover at a crew transition. Telegraph the Compact response to an early crossing and make the switch-room threat readable from the commander's view.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `commander-feedback` covers production handovers, rival notices and the repeatable playtest.

## Impact

Gate Six's scripted rules, HUD, crew art and native test instruments change. The same order path serves desktop, touch and headset controls.
