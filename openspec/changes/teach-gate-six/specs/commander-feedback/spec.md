## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Read a threat to the objective

The rival SHALL respond to a field section crossing toward the gate. The commander SHALL receive an audible muster signal and a persistent visual dispatch notice before the first rival section can occupy the switch room. An enemy hold SHALL display the seconds left to recover control. The commander SHALL be able to request a skippable, repeatable Gate Six practice shift and return to the same campaign tick. Each operation SHALL explain its objective, progress and a next action.

#### Scenario: Race to the switch room

- **WHEN** a player sends infantry early while an opposing section can muster
- **THEN** the Compact announces its relief party, travels to the objective and can interrupt an unsupported claim.

#### Scenario: Rehearse first field orders

- **WHEN** a new commander completes the first mill in practice and returns to duty
- **THEN** the lesson acknowledges the mill and restores the campaign including its unsaved progress.
