# Keep player preferences

## Why

Volume, legibility and simulation pace determine whether a commander can follow an entire operation. Players need to keep those choices across sessions.

## What Changes

Provide an editable settings draft, apply or cancel it, preview defaults and reapply committed preferences on launch, load and mission change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `player-settings` covers apply and retain player preferences.

## Impact

Native video and audio, WebGL rendering, browser storage and session UI.
