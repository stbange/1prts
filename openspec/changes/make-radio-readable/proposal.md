# Keep dispatch readable below deck

## Why

Essential crew acknowledgments compete with the score below deck. A commander needs to identify the speaker and review a message after it disappears.

## What Changes

Caption voice and non-speech signals, retain call history, distinguish map symbols by shape and attenuate the score and machinery during radio calls.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `field-audio` covers speech priority and ambience recovery.

### Modified Capabilities

- `field-command` covers distinguish reports from current observation.

## Impact

Radio mix, HUD, chart markers and local message history. The delta extends the existing field-command requirement.
