# Expose attribution and release notices

## Why

The playable distribution needs to carry its art credits, dependency notices and local-storage information wherever it is hosted.

## What Changes

Expose the credits and complete licenses from the session desk and the public release page. Describe storage, hosting and content suitability.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `runtime-content` covers distribute source and art with their licenses.

## Impact

Loose notices, credits reader and release page. The delta extends the existing runtime-content requirement.
