## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Hear the watch briefing

All operations SHALL provide a skippable dispatch from a named crew member describing the local stakes, objective and first decision. Players SHALL be able to reopen it after entering the field.

#### Scenario: Read the crossing dispatch after boarding

- **WHEN** the ferry commander reopens the Tide Window briefing
- **THEN** the pilot explains the required cargo, competing berth and possible investments without changing the crew assignment.
