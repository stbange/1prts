## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Distribute source and art with their licenses

Project code, including Lua and shaders, SHALL carry the MIT license. Original art and audio SHALL carry CC BY 4.0. The distribution SHALL provide the license texts and per-work credits identifying title, creator, source, license and modifications for the art. Credits SHALL expose original work and dependency notices at the session desk and on the public site and explain local saves, hosting and fictional conflict.

#### Scenario: Attribute a modified texture

- **WHEN** an original texture is adapted for a shipped livery
- **THEN** its credits identify the original creator and source, link CC BY 4.0 and describe the change, and both license texts are available in the distribution.

#### Scenario: Expose attribution and release notices

- **WHEN** a player opens the credits in the published distribution
- **THEN** the MIT and CC BY terms, SDL and Lua notices, original art credits and storage information are reachable.
