# Measure physical-device play

## Why

The crowded yard, moving ferry and two views inside Crown Station impose different costs. Desktop emulation cannot establish headset comfort or physical phone responsiveness.

## What Changes

Record completed frames, input-to-frame intervals, startup, memory and budget overruns for named routes. Identify each physical device and retain wearer observations.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `game-verification` covers exercise the rendered entry points.

## Impact

Native renderer probe, local browser profiler and hardware playtest records. The delta extends the existing game-verification requirement.
