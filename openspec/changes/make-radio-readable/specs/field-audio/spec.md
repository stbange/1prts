## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mix the watch radio

The score and location ambience SHALL attenuate by at least 6 dB while radio speech plays. After speech ends, both SHALL recover within one second at the normal or assisted simulation pace.

#### Scenario: A call arrives in the engine room

- **WHEN** the player receives a radio call while machinery and music are playing
- **THEN** both background streams are reduced during speech and their selected gains are restored afterwards.
