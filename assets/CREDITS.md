# Original game art

The 1stprts contributors created the Orra Basin terrain, crewed structures, vehicles, sprites, lettering, faction markings and radio cues. The models and cues can be regenerated with `tools/make_art.py` and `tools/campaign_art.py`. The radio dialogue was written for this game. The six recordings were synthesized and radio-filtered with `tools/crew_voices.py` using Carnegie Mellon University's Flite and the CMU US RMS, SLT and AWB voices. [Flite](https://www.festvox.org/flite/)

The original compartment fittings, plate markers and Crown lift were modeled for Orra Basin. The shift motif, apron machinery and engine-room loop were composed and synthesized for this game. All are CC BY 4.0, credited to the 1stprts contributors. The editable authoring sources are in `tools/rooms_art.py` and `tools/basin_music.py`.

The contributors drew the Cyrillic bitmap letters for the command slate in the same five-by-seven grid as the original Latin lettering. The editable glyph sheet and game captures use CC BY 4.0. [Glyph sheet](lang/cyrillic.txt) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
